FAQs

Good questions.

Do I need planning permission?

Usually not. Most conversions fall under permitted development. Some new builds, conservation areas or properties with covenants do need it. We check this for free on your survey.

Do I need Building Regulations approval?

Yes, every habitable conversion does. We handle the application and inspections, and you get a completion certificate at the end.

How long does a conversion take?

It depends on the size of the garage and what you'd like it to become. You'll get a clear timeline with your quote.

How much does it cost?

Conversions start from £12,000, and most fall between £12,000 and £25,000. Your exact fixed price depends on size, condition and finish, and follows the free survey.

Can I run a clinic or salon from my garage?

Yes. We build treatment rooms for aesthetics, beauty, hair and therapy businesses, with options like a separate client entrance and a hand-wash basin. Depending on how many clients you'll see, a change of use may need planning permission, so we check this for you on the survey.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Hassocks and work within about 20 miles, across BN, RH and TN postcodes, including Brighton & Hove, Burgess Hill, Haywards Heath, Lewes, Crawley and Horsham.

Will it be warm enough to use all year?

Yes. We insulate the floor, walls and roof to current Building Regulations and add heating, so it feels like any other room.

Can I keep some storage space?

Absolutely. Many clients convert the back two-thirds and keep a storage area or utility at the front.

Do you offer finance?

Yes, finance is available, subject to status. Ask us about monthly payment options at your free survey.
