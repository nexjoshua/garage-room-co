Planning & regulations

We deal with the paperwork.

Most garage conversions fall under permitted development, but every one needs Building Regulations approval. Covenants, conservation areas, new-build restrictions and running a business from home can change things. We check all of this before you commit to anything.

  • Permitted development checkPlus a planning application if needed.
  • Structural engineerFoundation checks and calculations for any steelwork.
  • Party wall adviceFor attached or shared garages.
